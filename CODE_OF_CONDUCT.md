# Code of conduct

Cloud Peek welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat people with respect, offer constructive feedback, and focus criticism on the work. Respect personal boundaries, identities, and privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing, and persistent disruptive behavior are not acceptable. These standards apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where people represent the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to Isaac Hatilima at isaachatilima@gmail.com. Include links and relevant context without exposing other people's private information publicly. Reports will be handled with care for the privacy of those involved; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, request changes in behavior, restrict participation, or ban participants depending on severity and repeated behavior. Affected participants can request reconsideration through the same contact. Maintainers are expected to follow these standards too.
